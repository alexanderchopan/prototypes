grantr

The app spine, clickable. Four tabs, the member popover, quick actions, and the two working trays.

Try this

  • Tap + → Send. Pick a recipient, choose tokens, enter an amount.
  • Press Add transfer — it queues and clears the form, so you can add a second recipient.
  • Watch the primary button: it reads Send until the count passes 1, then Send (n).
  • Tap the blue chip beside the title to switch sub-surface.

What's real

Layout, tokens, spacing, type and the tray state machines are transcribed from grantr-mobile-presentation.tsx. Row separators genuinely don't draw — ss.hairline is transparent.

Balances, contacts, quotes and activity are invented. No network, no wallet, no backend.

Not built

~25 other DetailTray variants (security policies, signers, devices, recovery, Fileverse), the remaining action routes, and the desktop docked variant.
